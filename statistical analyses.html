Statistical Analyses

Statistical Basics

Through a starter course of Statistics, I learned the basics and common tests used for statistical analytics. Probability calculations, normal and Poisson distributions, the Wilcoxon signed-rank test, t-test, Mann-Whitney-test, Chi²-goodness-of-fit-test, ANOVA-tables and lots of other tests and analyses were practiced through Excel, using mathematical principles to fully comprehend what is happening.
